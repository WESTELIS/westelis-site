BEFORE WE TALK

A few useful details.

What should I send if I want to sell a watch?

Send the brand, full reference number, asking price, and clear photographs of the dial, case, caseback, bracelet or strap, and any included accessories. Mention condition, service history, and whether you have the original box and papers. Keep serial numbers and personal information in a private conversation.

Can I ask about a specific reference?

Yes. Send the full reference if you have it, along with your preferred dial, bracelet or strap, approximate budget, and any requirements for condition or completeness.

How are payment and delivery arranged?

Payment and delivery terms are discussed and agreed directly for each transaction. Local meetings can be arranged at a bank or jeweler. Confirm all payment instructions directly with WESTELIS before sending funds.

Where can I see available watches?

Browse the Available Watches section or contact WESTELIS directly. Our Instagram account also features watches and updates. Availability can change, so confirm a watch is available before making plans to purchase.
