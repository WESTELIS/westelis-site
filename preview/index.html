How are payment and delivery arranged?

Terms are agreed directly for each transaction. Local meetings can be arranged at a bank or jeweler. Confirm all payment instructions directly with WESTELIS before sending funds.

Can I ask about a specific reference?

Yes. Send the full reference if you have it, along with your preferred dial, bracelet or strap, approximate budget, and any requirements for condition or completeness.

What should I send if I want to sell a watch?

The brand, full reference, asking price, and clear photos of the dial, case, caseback, bracelet and accessories. See how selling works.
